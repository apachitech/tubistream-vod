## 2. The Revenue Math: How Video Ads Make Money

Streaming video ad revenue is calculated using **CPM** (*Cost Per Mille* = Cost per 1,000 Ad Impressions).

### The Master Revenue Formula:

$$\text{Daily Ad Revenue} = \text{Total Impressions} \times \left(\frac{\text{Average CPM}}{1000}\right) \times \text{Fill Rate}$$

### How Impressions Accumulate per Viewer:
In a typical 90-minute movie session:
1. **Pre-Roll (at 0:00)**: 1 ad ($0.03)
2. **Mid-Roll 1 (at 15:00)**: 2 ads (double pod) ($0.06)
3. **Mid-Roll 2 (at 30:00)**: 2 ads ($0.06)
4. **Mid-Roll 3 (at 45:00)**: 2 ads ($0.06)
5. **Mid-Roll 4 (at 60:00)**: 2 ads ($0.06)
6. **Mid-Roll 5 (at 75:00)**: 2 ads ($0.06)
7. **Pause Ads (2 pauses)**: 2 shoppable impressions ($0.02)

**Total Ads Served in 1 Movie**: **13 Impressions**  
At an industry average **$25.00 CPM**, a single viewer watching one movie generates:
$$\frac{13 \text{ ads}}{1000} \times \$25.00 = \mathbf{\$0.325 \text{ per movie}}$$

If that viewer watches 20 movies in a month, **one single free user generates you $6.50/month**—without paying you a single penny out of their pocket!

## 3. The 4 Main Ways to Monetize Your Platform

### 🅰️ 1. Programmatic Video Ad Exchanges (Fastest to Start)
Connect your TubiStream VAST endpoint to automated video ad exchanges. Whenever an ad break occurs, advertisers bid in milliseconds in an auction to show their ad to your viewer.

- **Major SSPs (Supply-Side Platforms)**:
  - **Google Ad Manager 360 (GAM)**: Global brand inventory with high CTV fill rates.
  - **Magnite (formerly Telaria / Rubicon)**: The largest independent Connected TV ad exchange.
  - **FreeWheel (Comcast)**: Premium broadcast TV advertiser network.
  - **SpringServe / PubMatic**: High-performance video ad servers.
- **Typical CPM Range**: **$15.00 – $32.00 CPM**

---

### 🅱️ 2. Direct-Sold Brand Sponsorships (Highest Profit: 100% Margin)
Sell ad space directly to companies, skipping middlemen and ad network commission fees.

- **Examples**:
  - Local auto dealerships, beverage brands, tech companies, or mobile game publishers.
  - **Category Exclusivity**: An energy drink brand pays $5,000/month to be the exclusive advertiser during all action movies.
  - **Branded FAST Channels**: A fitness equipment brand pays a monthly sponsorship fee to host a 24/7 dedicated fitness channel.
- **Typical CPM Range**: **$35.00 – $65.00+ CPM**

---

### 🅲 3. FAST 24/7 Live Channel Revenue Splits
Run linear live channels (e.g., Action TV, Sci-Fi Central, Crime Time). FAST channels broadcast standard 14 to 16 minutes of commercials per broadcast hour:

- **Revenue Split Model**:
  - Partner provides content and takes 50% of the ad inventory (7 minutes/hour).
  - You (platform operator) keep 50% of the ad inventory (7 minutes/hour).
- **Earnings**: 1,000 concurrent viewers streaming a FAST channel generate **14,000 ad impressions every single hour**.

---

### 🅳 4. Shoppable Pause Ads & Affiliate Commerce
When viewers pause playback, TubiStream renders a non-intrusive card with a scannable **QR Code**.

- **How You Get Paid**:
  - **Cost Per Action (CPA) / Affiliate**: If a user scans the QR code and purchases a product, you earn a 10% to 30% commission (e.g., Amazon Associates, Impact, CJ Affiliate).
  - **Flat Sponsor Placement**: Brand pays $1,000/month to have their product featured on all pause overlays.

## 5. TubiStream Engine Features Built to Maximize Your Revenue

Your platform includes dedicated ad tech features specifically engineered to maximize profit:

1. **Double Ad Podding (`maxAdsPerBreak = 2`)**:
   - In **Admin CMS -> Ad Engine & Inventory**, switch *Max Ads per Pod* from 1 to 2.
   - Doubles your revenue at every single cue point without increasing viewer churn.

2. **Pre-Roll Guarantee (`prerollEnabled = true`)**:
   - Guarantees an immediate monetized impression the instant a viewer presses play, even if they only watch 5 minutes of a film.

3. **Dynamic Floor CPM Pricing**:
   - Set minimum floor rates (e.g. `$28.50`). Never let advertisers underpay for premium Connected TV screen space.

4. **Shoppable Pause Ads with Dynamic QR**:
   - Monetizes idle paused time without annoying the viewer.

5. **Audience Demographics & Telemetry**:
   - High-definition QoS telemetry and device breakdown metrics in the Admin Studio give you the exact proof-of-performance numbers required to win direct enterprise advertising contracts.

## 6. Step-by-Step: Connecting Real Ad Networks

To plug real advertisers into TubiStream, connect external ad tags into the platform's VAST engine:

### Option A: Google Ad Manager (GAM 360)
1. Register for a Google Ad Manager account.
2. Under **Inventory -> Video Ad Units**, generate a **VAST Tag URL**.
3. In TubiStream **Admin CMS -> Ad Engine & Inventory -> Create & Schedule New Ad Campaign**:
   - Paste the GAM VAST tag URL into the **Video Media Stream URL** field.
   - Set Floor CPM to `$25.00`.
   - Click **Deploy Ad Campaign**.
4. Every time an ad break triggers, TubiStream calls Google's live auction exchange and streams the winning commercial.

### Option B: Video Header Bidding (Prebid Server Video)
- Point your cue points to a Prebid Server video adapter.
- Multiple SSPs (Magnite, OpenX, PubMatic) bid against each other simultaneously in a millisecond auction; the highest bidder wins the ad spot.

## 7. Content Acquisition Strategy: Getting Movies Without Upfront Costs

You do not need millions of dollars to build a massive library. Here is how independent platforms acquire hundreds of films:

1. **Revenue Share Agreements (50/50 Net Ad Split)**:
   - Approach independent filmmakers, YouTube creators, or indie film distributors (e.g., Filmhub, Indie Rights).
   - You host their film for free.
   - When ads play during their movie, you split the ad earnings 50% / 50%. You incur **zero upfront risk or licensing cost**.
2. **Public Domain & Creative Commons Classics**:
   - Thousands of classic films, cult horror, documentaries, and golden-age series (like *Night of the Living Dead*, *Sherlock Holmes*, *Charade*) are 100% royalty-free and can be monetized legally forever.
3. **FAST Linear Content Syndication**:
   - Streamers can syndicate existing FAST feeds directly using HLS live streams provided by content partners under shared ad breaks.

The most lucrative streaming business model is **Hybrid (AVOD + SVOD)**:

```
                                  [ ALL VIEWERS ]
                                         |
                 +-----------------------+-----------------------+
                 |                                               |
                 v                                               v
         [ 90% Free Viewers ]                            [ 10% VIP Subscribers ]
                 |                                               |
        Watches Movies with Ads                         Pays $9.99/mo for Ad-Free
                 |                                               |
       Generates $5 - $15/mo                           Generates Guaranteed MRR
      in Continuous Ad Revenue                       (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
```

- **90% of your audience stays Free**: They watch movies with ads, earning you continuous ad revenue month after month.
- **10% of heavy users hate commercials**: They click **"Upgrade to VIP"** and pay you **$9.99/month**.
- TubiStream automatically toggles ad suppression for VIP members using `vipAdFreeBypass`, giving you **two separate revenue streams from the same platform**.
